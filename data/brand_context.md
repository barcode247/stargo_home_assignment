# Stargo Brand Context (Marketing Brain excerpt)

Synthetic excerpt for this exercise (v2). Treat it as the only approved source of claims about Stargo.

## BC-1 Positioning
Stargo builds agentic AI that turns messy enterprise documents, emails and datasets into trusted operational transactions across freight, logistics and trade finance. Humans stay in control: agents draft, people approve.

## BC-2 Who we serve
Freight forwarders, carriers, 3PLs, air cargo and ground handlers, customs brokers, shippers with material freight spend, and trade-finance teams.

## BC-3 Products
- **BC-3a Rate Quoting Agent.** Reads emailed rate requests, pulls the relevant tariffs and contracts, and drafts a quote for a pricing analyst to approve.
- **BC-3b Document Extraction.** Extracts structured fields from bills of lading, air waybills, invoices and customs paperwork into the TMS or ERP, with validation.
- **BC-3c Invoice and Contract Reconciliation.** Matches carrier invoices to contracted rates and flags discrepancies for review.
- **BC-3d Trade Document Checking.** Validates trade documents for finance teams before funds move.

## BC-4 Proof points (illustrative, approved for this exercise only)
- **PP-1.** A European forwarder pilot cut median spot-quote turnaround from about 3 hours to under 15 minutes.
- **PP-2.** Document extraction pilots reached 97% field-level accuracy on bills of lading after human review.
- **PP-3.** An air cargo handler removed manual AWB re-keying at two stations.

## BC-5 Claims we never make
- No customer names unless listed as approved (none are approved here).
- No ROI or accuracy figures other than PP-1 to PP-3.
- No statements about competitors' products, pricing or weaknesses. A competitor's name may appear only where it repeats the buyer's own question (a title or an FAQ question). The answer itself is about Stargo.
- Stargo is not a carrier, broker, forwarder or travel company. We do not move freight.

## BC-6 Voice
Plain, specific, operator to operator. Short sentences. No hype words such as "revolutionary" or "game-changing".

## BC-7 GEO content contract
Articles written for AI answer engines must have: a title that matches the buyer's question; a 40 to 60 word direct answer as the first paragraph; 3 to 5 sections with descriptive headings; an FAQ block of 3 to 5 questions; every factual claim tagged with the BC or PP id it comes from; a suggested schema.org type.
